# Co-Creative Healing — Brand & Design System

## Source status

This document synthesizes the November 2026 Brand & Website Brief, In My Own Words, and My Story PDFs. Text marked **Confirm** needs Stephanie's decision before publication. Design directions describe the intended website, not fixed product requirements.

## 1. Brand Identity & Philosophy

- **Practice Name:** Co-Creative Healing
- **Practitioner:** Stephanie Shrum (Bodyworker & Healing Facilitator)
- **Location:** Confirm whether the public site may say "Upper Ojai, California." Until then, use "Ojai, California" or omit the locality.
- **Core Tagline:** _"Come whole. Create together. Leave expanded."_ (Used alongside the logo everywhere; set in Cormorant Garamond italic)
- **Tagline Sub-copy:** _"You are already whole. Together, we create the space for you to leave as a more expanded version of yourself."_
- **Home Hero Headline:** _"All of you is welcome."_
- **Philosophy Headline:** _"Whole on our own. Something new together."_

### Brand Pillars

- **Already Whole:** Every person is uniquely whole; we have only forgotten the path back.
- **Co-Creation:** The client's life experience and Stephanie's combine into a wisdom neither has alone.
- **Transformation:** Clients arrive whole and may expand into a version of themselves they did not know was possible.
- **All of You Is Welcome:** Shadow and light, the sacred and the playful, belong to the whole.
- **Your Difference Is Your Gift:** Like the four-leaf clover (a treasured "mutation"), what makes a person stand out or not fit in is what makes them unique.
- **The Gift of Noticing:** Stephanie brings the sensitivity that found one clover in a field of thousands to what asks for attention in a client's body and words.
- **Invite, Don't Force:** Dialogue with the body; meeting tissues and consciousness where they are rather than forcing release.
- **Grounded Presence:** Humility, reverence, and deep intuition—never fixing, always meeting people where they are.

### Voice & Visual Guardrails

- **Tone:** Calm, grounded, warm, easy to navigate, and informative, with a touch of New Orleans and travel spark on the About page. Use plain words over jargon.
- **Strictly Avoid:** Wellness clichés such as _"mind, body, spirit"_ as a tagline, lotus flowers, hand prints, and cloud backgrounds.

## 2. Logo Specifications

- **Visual Concept:** A four-leaf clover with a gentle earth-ink stem. Two leaves are **Sage** and two are **Terracotta**, representing two whole individuals (client and practitioner, mentor and student, or community) coming together to create something new.
- **Usage Rules:**
    - Maintain generous whitespace around the mark.
    - Place on Cream (`#F7F2EA`) or very light backgrounds.
    - Can stand alone as a favicon, social avatar, or section stamp.

---

## 3. Color Palette (CSS / Tailwind Tokens)

Every color is drawn from Stephanie's healing studio. Lead with Cream and Sage, use Terracotta for buttons and highlights, and keep Misty Sea and Soft Brass as small accents.

| Token Name   | Hex Code  | Role & Usage Rules                                                                    |
| :----------- | :-------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `cream`      | `#F7F2EA` | Primary page background (minimal, airy, natural)                          |
| `sage`       | `#8FA176` | Main brand color, soft section backgrounds, logo leaves                |
| `terracotta` | `#A35F45` | Primary CTA buttons, highlights, logo leaves, single Home feature band |
| `earth-ink`  | `#4A3F36` | Primary body copy, headings, and logo stem                                   |
| `honey-wood` | `#D9B77E` | Warm accents and section dividers                                            |
| `jute`       | `#C9AE85` | Subtle textured backgrounds for story sections                            |
| `misty-sea`  | `#A9BCC4` | Occasional thin accent or link color only (**never** large backgrounds)   |
| `soft-brass` | `#B8954A` | Tiny touches: thin horizontal rules, hairlines, and icons                 |

## 4. Typography (Google Fonts)

- **Headings & Logo Wordmark:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Elegant, soft serif; use _Italic_ for taglines)
- **Body Text & Buttons:** `Jost` (Clean, friendly sans-serif)

---

## 5. Global UX, Conversion & Privacy Rules

- **Persistent Dual CTAs:** Every page must feature two clear action buttons
- **Booking flow:** A visitor can choose "Let's talk first" for a short phone conversation or "Request a session" with a short intake. Stephanie confirms the match before sharing arrival details.
- **Privacy:** Never publish the studio address, a map that reveals it, or photos that reveal the location. Share the address only after the session is confirmed.
- **Mutual match:** A client can stop a session at any time if it does not feel like a match and owes nothing for that session.
- **Newsletter:** Include the newsletter in the footer and on the Mentorship and Ojai Hundred pages.
- **Social and search:** Link Facebook and Instagram in the footer. Use natural search terms in page titles and copy. A service-area Google Business Profile remains a compliance question.
